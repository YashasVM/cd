Implementation order and pass criteria

  1. Profile the ordinary transfer path.

    Measure direct assembly, source reads, crypto, queue waits, sink writes, and final save. Compare HEAD/current browser windows and repeat on two devices.

    Gate: seven paired trials per candidate; actual route bytes, integrity, completion, and memory recorded.
  2. Reduce ordinary browser overhead.

    Use one bounded assembly buffer, consume ACKs continuously, and wake direct sends on low-buffer events. Test each change separately.

    Target: ≥20% better median completion in the constrained case; no unexplained slowdown over 5% in other core cases.
  3. Pipeline durable resume transfers.

    Keep one OPFS worker handle. Test a 4 MiB send window with 1–2 MiB durable batches. Coordinate the current 2 MiB inbox and negotiate batching with old peers.

    Milestones: ≥10 MiB/s at 100 ms and ≥5 MiB/s at 300 ms on the controlled fast host. ACK only committed, recoverable bytes.
  4. Remove remaining CPU and network limits.

    Compare faster bounded-memory streaming hashes. Tune SCTP receive credit and direct promotion only where profiles show a limit. Add direct transport to resume after its pipeline works.

    Preserve ordered records, fresh nonces, bounded memory, and final persisted-file verification.
